Selected work

Simulation

Romania Merit-Order Simulator

A dispatch model of the Romanian electricity market, built as an independent cross-check on solar and battery-storage investment cases. Python/FastAPI backend, React frontend, calibrated against historical OPCOM day-ahead prices.

  • Cross-border coupling with Hungary, Bulgaria and Serbia
  • Corrected overstated transfer limits on three interconnectors
  • Reconciled IRENA against ENTSO-E for undercounted solar capacity
  • Backtests to ~13–15% deviation in normal years; the 2022 crisis year degrades to 28–44%, which traced back to gas and CO₂ anchors rather than the dispatch logic
Python · FastAPI · React · pandas · ENTSO-E · OPCOM
Data pipeline

Turkey Macro Dashboard

An automated pipeline pulling macroeconomic series from 18 mapped institutional sources on a daily schedule, with monthly e-mail reports.

  • Reverse-engineered keyless TÜİK endpoints and a two-step Treasury API pattern
  • Bulletin-feed polling so fetches follow real publication dates
  • Last-known-value fallback when a source goes down
Python · GitHub Actions · EVDS · TÜİK · FRED

About

Most of what I do sits between software engineering and empirical modelling: take messy institutional data, build something that reproduces reality closely enough to be useful, then find out where it fails.

Right now that means electricity market simulation for a renewable energy investment firm — dispatch models, cross-border coupling, backtesting against historical prices. The interesting part is rarely the code. It's deciding which assumptions the data can support, and being honest about the ones it can't.

I also ship full-stack products end to end. I work in English and Turkish, and I'm increasingly drawn to technical AI safety — particularly evaluations and how agentic systems behave in practice.
